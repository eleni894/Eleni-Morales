Die-Less Hydroforming Research

The last time I visited my family in Greece, I took a piece of sea glass from the beach, knowing I would one day be able to make it into a necklace.

I designed and CNCed an aluminum necklace holder to resin cast the sea glass in. It was such a great experience CAMing and machining something with a non-trivial geometry.

I’m very proud of the final result! It looks exactly how I envisioned it. I am glad I was able to push the boundaries of CAM to create this!
